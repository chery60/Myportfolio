## Introduction

Recur Club is a financial platform that provides hassle-free financing for startups. It offers a variety of benefits, including:

Quick and easy access to capital: Recur Club can provide startups with the capital they need to grow and succeed, quickly and easily.<br />
Flexible repayment options: Recur Club offers flexible repayment options that are tailored to the needs of each startup.<br />
No collateral required: Recur Club does not require any collateral, making it a good option for startups with limited assets.Expert support: Recur Club provides expert support to startups throughout the financing process.

## Back Story

The company product is one of the products among the investors portal, admin, and insights products which was taking shape after 1 year of operations. Initially, the customer success team and business developments used to handle the startup that came for debt financing. But as the company was approached by a huge number of startups than companies in onboarding was getting very difficult to handle so a project was taken up to build a seamless product experience for the two personas one was the founder and the other was the finance manager's persona.

## Using Qualitative Methods

There was a process set by the organization to talk to companies regularly and take timely feedback on the product's journey<br />
After going through the hot jar recording and the mix panel user flow analysis I have made a couple of questions as a list of questions to be asked along with understanding the user pain points.

## Example of list of questions

Understanding the signup process.<br />
Where do get all the required files to complete the onboarding?<br />
How do they add the finance manager to complete the onboarding process?<br />
What time does it take to get all those files?<br />
What are the tasks that people are doing after post-listing?<br />
How they are making Repayments?<br />
Why do they use payments and payouts? and how frequently<br />
What do people do in the collections module?<br />
How do they make financial reports using our product?<br />
How they are tracking their repayments? etc<br />
Conducted User interviews and found all the pain points and understood their expectations.

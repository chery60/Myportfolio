## Introduction

Oracle’s Food and Beverage Global Business Unit provides a comprehensive suite of enterprise software solutions designed for various restaurant types, including quick-service restaurants (QSRs), fast casuals, limited service restaurants, and stadiums. Our current system connects the Enterprise Management Console (EMC), Point of Sale (POS), and Kitchen Display System (KDS) to create a seamless food ordering experience. However, we’ve identified a gap in the ordering process, particularly for QSRs and similar venues. To address this, we’re introducing a new entity—the kiosk. This solution is aimed at increasing orders, influencing consumer buying behavior, and driving higher engagement.

## Back Story

Oracle was building a new guest self-ordering experience with a kiosk, which deals with ordering the food to pick up the ordered food. The hardware was also built by Oracle FBGBU unit. Everything was to be built from scratch.

## Identification of Problem

The restaurants and food courts at stadiums used to get so many orders daily. here is the list of identified problems —<br />
1. The staff to serve the orders was less.<br />
2. Order entry errors.<br />
3. The time to take orders was very high.<br />
4. People with disabilities found it hard to give orders<br />
5. The person who is introverted in nature was unable to order their food.<br />
6. The delivery of ordered food items was a pain.<br />
7. Making payments on receiving orders was a pain<br />
8. Persons with language issues (mostly foreigners) are unable to order food efficiently.

### How could building a Kiosk would reduce the amount of effort and solve the problems?

1. The effort to take orders will be drastically reduced as a result the less staff is required to take orders and they can focus on delivering the ordered food.<br />
2. As the Person himself was ordering the food taking orders with errors was reduced.<br />
3. With the kiosk, the ordering will be a very fast and smooth experience.<br />
4. Persons with introverted nature can order on their own.<br />
5. The delivery of food will be easy as the Kiosk gives an estimated time to get the food delivered and even gets a notification on mobile on food being ready to be delivered.<br />
6. Using a kiosk, the payments are also made easy for the ordered food.
